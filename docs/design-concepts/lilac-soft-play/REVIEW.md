# OkayKaba - Soft Play concept

The user selected the lilac/purple reference palette on November 17, 2026, replacing the earlier blue direction for this concept exploration. These boards were created with the built-in image-generation tool using the user's attached UI and palette screenshots as references. They are original proposed screens, not screenshots of implemented functionality.

## Visual system

- Electric violet #5500FF for disciplined emphasis; lilac #A67AFF and pale lavender #D5C4FA as the signature palette.
- Dark ink #17141E, warm white and black primary controls.
- Small soft-cyan, pink and warm-cream supporting surfaces; no green or sage branding.
- A subtle cyan/lilac top wash, thin dark outlines, moderate rounded corners and minimal shadows.
- Original thought-cloud illustration family; preserve OkayKaba branding rather than the reference's mindcare name or mascot.
- The existing typography can remain Plus Jakarta Sans. Small generated labels, quote punctuation and date art are illustrative; production calendar math and consistent mood labels remain authoritative.

## Boards

1. Home, Daily Inspiration, Talk it through.
2. Calendar, Journal, To-do, Your space.

Quotes are the default daily inspiration content. Bible verses are optional and initially disabled. The AI chat remains a proposed separate integration: it must be identified as AI support rather than therapy, offer support-resource navigation and user-controlled sharing, and never automatically read journals. The depicted sharing toggle is a concept; a specific journal-entry consent flow must be designed before implementing external sharing.

## Motion

Soft card transitions, a controlled reply reveal, expanding active navigation, mood-expression and gauge movement, calendar day details, and task rows moving with the completion ring. Respect reduced motion. The static boards do not demonstrate finished animation timing.

## Status

Only design artifacts were created. No running-app theme, screens, Supabase schema, auth behavior or AI backend was changed, committed or pushed. Existing AGENTS.md still describes the currently implemented blue theme; update it when the approved implementation adopts this design.

Exact generation prompts are saved alongside these boards. Some depicted controls, such as notifications and AI chat, are proposals rather than connected features.
