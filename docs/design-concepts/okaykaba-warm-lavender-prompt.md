# OkayKaba concept board

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The board illustrates Home, Journal, Mood Calendar, and Insights. Its example names and check-ins are mock content.

## Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. A high-fidelity design board for OkayKaba, an original Android wellbeing app. Four flat, straight-on 390x844 phone UI screens side by side, no perspective or hardware. Warm off-white #FCFAF8 60%, soft lavender #EEE7F8 30%, deep violet #5423A6 10%, ink #231B30. Plus Jakarta Sans style. Quiet editorial journal aesthetic, precise spacing, thin lavender rules, matte surfaces, small touches of frosted glass only on floating navigation, minimal shadows. No mascots, illustrations, photography, decorative badges or excessive gradients. Screen 1 Home: small OkayKaba wordmark, 'Hi, Alex', large lavender check-in panel with 'How are you feeling today?' and five custom purple mood faces labeled Low, Meh, Okay, Good, Great; horizontal swipeable activity tiles (Journal and Self-care and partial To-do), smaller quote section. Screen 2 Journal: 'Journal', a writing panel labeled 'New entry', ruled divider, 'Save entry', and previous entry rows. Screen 3 Calendar: 'Mood Calendar', Today button, Monthly and Year in Pixels segmented selector, Monday-first November 2026 calendar using five purple faces on recorded days, tiny empty circles, selected date card below. Screen 4 Insights: 'Insights', a lavender seven-day mood panel, separate simple stat rows for journal and daily tasks. Phosphor-like line icons. Show consistent bottom dock with Home, Insights, Calendar, Account icons and an active-only text pill. Design to guide a real Expo implementation, readable labels, generous touch targets and understated rounded rectangles.
