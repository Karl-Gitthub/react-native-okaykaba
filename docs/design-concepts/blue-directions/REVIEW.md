These original visual proposals were generated with the built-in image-generation tool. They are for comparison; no app code, theme tokens, backend, Git commit or live chatbot was changed. Sample quotes and conversations illustrate layout, not production functionality.

Small generated label differences are not specifications; implementation must use one consistent set of mood labels and exact accessible color tokens.

## Daily Inspiration

User preference: motivational quotes by default; Bible verses only as an optional setting, OFF initially. One curated item per local day, bookmark/save, sharing, and a reflection prompt that opens the journal. Credit verified authors/sources and identify Bible translations. Use licensed or public-domain material where appropriate. Do not fabricate author attribution or scriptural citations. The sample line on these boards is original OkayKaba reflection copy, not an attributed quotation.

## Talk it through

Proposed separate in-app AI conversation for everyday struggles and reflection. Entry from Home avoids crowding the bottom navigation. Suggested modes: Just listen, One small step, Help me reflect. Clearly identify AI and its limits; do not present it as a therapist, diagnosis, treatment or an emergency service.

Before implementation: choose a provider and usage budget, keep credentials server-side, define retention/deletion controls, explain what is sent externally and obtain appropriate consent, and add abuse controls and rate limits. Do not automatically read journals, mood history or verses into a conversation. Any Save to journal action must be explicit. Build and test support-resource navigation and high-risk conversation handling before a public release. No claim of clinical effectiveness is made.
