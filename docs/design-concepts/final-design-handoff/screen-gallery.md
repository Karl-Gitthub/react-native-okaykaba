# OkayKaba screen gallery

The approved design boards are listed in app order. Open a board for the full image, or open [the visual gallery](gallery.html) from a local checkout to see the boards on one page. [Read the design handoff](review.md) for navigation and state behavior.

## First-run and account access

- [Splash and optional breathing](../revised-first-run-flow/v2/01-opening-breath.png). Splash, invitation, inhale and exhale. The light artwork documents the breathing sequence; the dark entry board below is the visual reference for the invitation.
- [Pause and reduced motion](../revised-first-run-flow/v2/02-paused-welcome-demo.png) (selected panel or panels). Pause and Resume, plus the static quiet-moment option.
- [Dark breathing and email entry](../full-app-design/01-opening-and-email.png). Breathing invitation, email sign-up, sign-in and verification entry.
- [Welcome and Google handoff](../revised-first-run-flow/v6-account-recovery/01-idle-opening-cancelled.png). The approved account-choice layout, the pending Google handoff, and neutral cancellation.
- [Google failure and stalled handoff](../revised-first-run-flow/v6-account-recovery/02-failure-stalled.png). A failed connection restores choices. A stalled handoff offers Cancel.
- [Email sign-up and sign-in recovery](02-sign-up-and-sign-in-recovery.png). Validation, pending requests, uncertain sign-up and incorrect credentials.
- [Verification recovery](03-verification-recovery.png). Checking, still pending, connection failure, expired link and verified without an app session.
- [Reset request and resend](06-reset-request-and-resend.png). Forgot password, resend pending, accepted and failed, plus verified with a session.
- [Change email](../revised-first-run-flow/v7-first-run-bridges/02-dark-change-email.png). Editing, guarded commit, uncertain outcome and confirmed failure.
- [Password reset recovery](../revised-first-run-flow/v7-first-run-bridges/03-dark-password-recovery.png). Sending, acknowledgement, reset request failure and password update failure.
- [Reset link and access recovery](04-password-link-and-access-recovery.png). Expired reset link, new password, updating, confirmed success and expired email-change session.
- [Demo entry](../revised-first-run-flow/v7-first-run-bridges/01-dark-demo.png) (selected panel or panels). The device-storage explanation precedes Continue in demo. Back returns to the entry screen.
- [Name, practice and goal](../revised-first-run-flow/v5-dark-account/01-default-setup.png). Optional name, a practice check-in that creates no record, and optional goal.
- [Enlarged-text setup](../revised-first-run-flow/v5-dark-account/02-enlarged-text-only.png). Wrapped names, larger labels and the mood choice grid.
- [Demo ready](../revised-first-run-flow/v7-first-run-bridges/01-dark-demo.png) (selected panel or panels). Demo setup finishes locally, then Open Today starts the first real check-in.
- [Account setup completion](../revised-first-run-flow/v7-first-run-bridges/04-dark-account-completion.png). Local saving, local failure and the three account sync outcomes.
